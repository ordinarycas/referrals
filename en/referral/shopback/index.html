What is ShopBack?

Earn cash back at partner merchants through ShopBack; sign up with the invite link and both you and your friend can get a referral reward.

How do I sign up with the ShopBack referral link?

  1. Select “Sign up” to open the invite link and register a ShopBack account.
  2. Complete orders totalling $800 or more at partner merchants through ShopBack.
  3. Wait until the cash back status becomes “Confirmed”, and update your bank account details for withdrawals.

Notes and risks

This link does not include a code; just sign up with the link. According to the source, your friend must complete all of the following within 180 days of registering: sign up with the invite link, code or email, place orders totalling $800 or more, have the cash back for those orders become “Confirmed”, and update their bank account details. Only then does the referral count as “qualified” and both sides receive the reward. The actual conditions and amounts are set by ShopBack.
