What is ether.fi Cash Card?

The ether.fi Cash Card (officially Ether.fi Cash Card) is a crypto credit card from the Ethereum staking protocol ether.fi, built around a non-custodial wallet, staking yield and cash back on spending.

Tiers and rewards (compiled from a third-party article; ether.fi's official announcements prevail):

  • Core: free; 3% cash back on the first $2,000 per month; 1 free virtual card, physical card $50.
  • Luxe: unlocked with 5,000 spend points, $15,000 deposited in Liquid, 30,000 staked ETHFI or a $199 annual fee; 3% on the first $10,000 per month; 10% category cash back on selected subscriptions (capped at $10 per month); airport lounge single-entry vouchers twice a year; 2 free virtual cards and 1 free physical card.
  • Pinnacle: unlocked with 25,000 spend points, $100,000 deposited in Liquid, 150,000 staked ETHFI or a $999 annual fee; 3% on the first $50,000 per month; 20% category cash back (capped at $20 per month); 10 free ATM withdrawals per month; unlimited lounge vouchers (one per day); a dedicated account manager.
  • VIP (invitation only): 4% on the first $50,000 per month; 30% category cash back (capped at $30 per month); 10 free ATM withdrawals per month; dedicated support.
